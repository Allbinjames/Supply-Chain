# 📦 Supply Chain & Logistics Optimization – Datacom

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focused on reducing late deliveries for **Datacom**, a global e-commerce company, to improve customer satisfaction and revenue.

---

## 🎯 Situation
High rates of late deliveries were negatively impacting customer experience and operational efficiency.

---

## ⚙️ Actions Performed

### 1. Data Manipulation
- Processed and cleaned complex logistics and delivery datasets

### 2. Interactive Monitoring
- Developed an interactive **Power BI dashboard** for real-time delivery tracking

### 3. Root Cause Analysis
- Analyzed supply chain flow to identify delivery bottlenecks
- Evaluated performance across departments

---

## 📊 Key Results
- Identified that **54% of late deliveries** were caused by **internal departmental delays**

---

## 🔍 Conclusion & Recommendations
Improving warehouse efficiency and strengthening internal communication protocols would significantly reduce late deliveries.

---

## 🛠️ Technologies Used
- Power BI  
- Supply Chain Analytics  
- Data Manipulation
